How the regions are split

Southeast is the 24 correspondent schools, plus other boys teams in those same counties. Southwest is the Kanawha Valley and the western coalfields. Northwest is the panhandle, the Ohio Valley, and north-central. Northeast is the eastern panhandle and the Potomac highlands. Those three follow county lines, because the correspondent lists for them were not in the file. A game shows on every region tab that has a team in it. Shady Spring still has no December game posted.
